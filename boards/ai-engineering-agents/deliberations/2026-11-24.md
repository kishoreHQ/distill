## Disagreements

Saboo's zero-employee cron company [shubham-saboo S6] is a wider licence than Steinberger's four-check gate [peter-steinberger S8]. Wrigley's unread approve [mckay-wrigley S17] conflicts with Theo's "loop until they agree" [theo-browne S5]. Srinivas's "don't say what you didn't retrieve" [aravind-srinivas S7] is a tighter speech rule than an unattended cron that messages people.

## Synthesis

The overlapping public advice is to keep the harness explicit, ground claims in retrieved notes, and put a test or a second model in front of an autonomous edit. It is not to staff a company with unattended agents, and it is not to compete with a lab on a general assistant. For this operator, the smallest build that uses the overlap is a cited runbook helper with a human gate on any change to a repo.

## Recommended next action

Write a one-page spec for a helper that answers only from a folder of runbooks and refuses when the folder does not contain the answer. Add a checklist that treats fetched pages as untrusted. Do not start a general chatbot, and do not quote Perplexity's revenue.
