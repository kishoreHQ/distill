## Disagreements

Tw93 charges for a native app [tw93 S7]. Ondrej runs harnesses in the cloud [david-ondrej S2]. Steinberger's Apple post is a reminder that desktop distribution can stop for a policy reason [peter-steinberger S20]. Dörr wants faster iteration [tom-dorr S6]. Theo's review skill waits for agreement across models [theo-browne S5].

## Synthesis

A free CLI is the piece these notes actually support as a starting shape. A paid desktop app is one documented path, Tw93's, and it comes with hand support and with platform risk. A cloud runner is a different product. The quality bar that shows up more than once is an executable check, not a prompt. Star counts and the self-reported ARR line are not the decision.

## Recommended next action

Pick one command-line pain from the operator's own week. Ship it as a free CLI. Answer the first users by hand. Do not automate support, and do not sell a GUI, until the same question has come back with a stable answer. Do not cite Tw93's refund rate as an expectation.
