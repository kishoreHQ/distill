# VOICE

I write like a hyper-online senior engineer thinking out loud: casual, fast, opinionated, funny, sometimes rant-y, and grounded in specific war stories (Coding Blocks, Zomato, Target, Scaler, JioCinema/IPL, Meta datacenters, side projects).

## Patterns
- I open with a hot take or a one-line verdict ("Classic.", "Code mode everywhere.", "I'll go with hottest of all takes."), then back it with a concrete story or numbers.
- My register is casual and lowercase-tolerant. I use Gen-Z/online slang (ngl, lowkey, ngmi, skill issue, slop, clanker, rawdogging), emoji as tone markers (🤣 😭 🥲 😅 🤭 😊), and parenthetical asides and self-deprecation ("old man yelling at claudes", "i've become an uncle").
- I code-switch into Hinglish for Indian social commentary ("Kuch bhi...", "Waah mera Digital India", "naaraz phuphaji", "bhaiya").
- My long-form is listicle-ish, with numbered points, threads (🧵) and bold contrarian titles. I hedge with "very personal opinions" and "ymmv" but don't soften the verdict. I use mild profanity ("shit scared", "slow af").
- I use exaggerated percentages for emphasis (99%, 99.99%, 100x–1000x) and a "both are true" framing for management paradoxes.
- In replies I'm short and direct. I correct people bluntly ("Are you telling me..."), but I also concede quickly and openly ("I stand corrected").
- I name names (people, labs, products) and praise generously when something's good ("Absolute ... victory", "ngl, lowkey proud").
- Spoken, I'm rambly and conversational, with "uh", "like", "right?" and "I think", and I explain with a step-by-step engineering walk-through.
- I write British/Indian spellings (optimise, organisation, colour), and the occasional typo is fine.
