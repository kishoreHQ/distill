# Cross-profile themes

Seeded from the 2026-11-24 import. Each item cites a profile source. A theme is a pattern in those public posts, not a finding about people who were not read.

## The harness is the product

Theo separates the harness from the chat interface [theo-browne S4]. The ledger's description of Tw93's article says a must-obey rule has to be a script that really runs [tw93 S3]. Srinivas describes a chair model over several models [aravind-srinivas S17] and a safe-fetch score for pages an agent reads [aravind-srinivas S16]. Pavan's pinned article is about harness engineering rather than the prompt [pavan-belagatti S1].

## Prove the motion by hand, then automate

Vatsal writes that automation cannot replace figuring out what works [vatsal-sanghvi S3]. The Mole essay's ledger description says support was manual until a question repeated [tw93 S4]. Levels, by contrast, already runs cron-scheduled LLM scripts and says he has zero employees [pieter-levels S4]. The theme is the order of operations, not a claim that every person in the corpus agrees.

## Distribution is a large share of the work, and it is often also the product

Ondrej's transcript excerpt says at least half the time should go to distribution [david-ondrej S6]. Koe says marketing is most of a creative's success [dan-koe S9]. Finn's growth product and his reply advice point the same way [alex-finn S6]. Several of these accounts sell communities or guides. The profile files record that conflict where the bundle did.

## Multi-model checks show up more than once

Theo's tstack skill loops until three models agree [theo-browne S5]. Srinivas describes a council with a chair model [aravind-srinivas S17]. Ondrej posts that using models from different labs on one project widens the idea set [david-ondrej S8]. Saboo posts an advisor, orchestrator, and worker split across model names [shubham-saboo S9]. The names of the models differ by post and should not be copied as a current shopping list.

## Solo operators describe cron and folders, not headcount

Levels' essay is explicit about worker scripts on a schedule [pieter-levels S4]. Saboo describes roles as folders and job descriptions as markdown, with cron [shubham-saboo S6]. Dörr's showcase site is rebuilt by scheduled GitHub Actions and refuses manual suggestions [tom-dorr S16]. These are operating notes. They are not evidence that the same setup produces the same revenue for someone else.

Levels' hit-rate post says 4 of 70+ projects made money [pieter-levels S8]. Piyush's day-104 post states a $694 MRR line [piyush-sachdeva S1]. Finn's bio states a $300k ARR line [alex-finn S1]. The batch summaries flag these as self-reported. This file does not average them.

## Platform owners can enter the niche, and platforms can stop a release

Piyush writes that Claude shipped an Instagram scheduler similar to his tool [piyush-sachdeva S8], and that a dependent tool needs the platform's approval [piyush-sachdeva S4]. Steinberger posted that an Apple developer agreement stopped his OSS releases [peter-steinberger S20].
