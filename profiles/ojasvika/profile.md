## E. Implementation playbooks

### Playbook 1 — Watch-them-onboard sessions (derived from [S5][S6])
- **Problem:** founders guess at onboarding friction.
- **Preconditions:** working build; 2 target users who've never seen it.
- **Tools:** screen/room, notes, simple survey.
- **Steps:** 1) Invite 2 non-users matching ICP. 2) Ask them to onboard without help; stay silent. 3) Note every hesitation. 4) Fix top 3 issues within a week. 5) Repeat weekly; ask "how would you feel if this stopped working?".
- **Expected output:** prioritized onboarding fixes + PMF signal.
- **Cost:** snacks/time. **Time:** 2 h/week. **Complexity:** low.
- **Risks:** small sample; friendly-audience bias.
- **Success metric:** onboarding completion rate; % "very disappointed" answers.
