# Prompt: run-board

Run the simulated board `<board-id>` on this question:

> <question>

Start the file with this sentence, unchanged:

SIMULATED ADVISORY BOARD. The real people named here have not joined this board, have not been contacted, and have not endorsed DISTILL, Kishore, or any recommendation in this file. Positions below are paraphrases of their public posts and pages, with citations. They are not quotes of private advice.

Then write, in order: Question, Positions, Disagreements, Synthesis, Recommended next action, Confidence.

For each member, cite `profiles/<id>` sources that exist. Quote the stored excerpt or paraphrase it and give the URL. If the member has no source on the question, say so.

Do not write in the member's voice as if they were in the room. Do not convert self-reported revenue into a goal.
